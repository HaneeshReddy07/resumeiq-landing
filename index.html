How the resume analyzer works

A focused three-step process to help you prepare for a specific opportunity.

Step 01

Choose a text-based PDF

Upload a selectable-text resume PDF. Scanned image-only PDFs are not supported.

Step 02

Add the target job

Paste the job description so the analysis can compare your resume with that role.

Step 03

Review the report

Check the match estimate, evidence, skill gaps, and suggestions before deciding what to change.

AI feedback is a starting point

ResumeIQ provides estimates, not official results from an employer's applicant tracking system. Verify every suggested detail, metric, skill, date, and achievement before using it.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before using an AI resume analyzer.

Does ResumeIQ guarantee an ATS score or interview?

No. Match scores are AI estimates and are not official scores from an employer's ATS. They cannot guarantee an interview or job.

What file types are supported?

ResumeIQ currently accepts text-based PDF resumes. Scanned or image-only PDFs are not supported because OCR is not enabled.

What happens to resume information?

Selectable resume text and the job description are sent to Google Gemini for analysis. Signed-in reports are saved to the user's private account history; guest reports remain in the current session. See the full privacy notice.

Can I use suggested wording as-is?

Review suggestions first. Do not add a skill, result, qualification, or experience claim unless it is true and you can support it.
